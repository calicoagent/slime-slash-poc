HOW TO PLAY
Move mouse / drag finger to swing your sword through slimes. Tap or click to use your WEAPON SKILL (once its Sphere path node is unlocked). Sphere Points come from level-ups, bounties, Kings (+1), Emperors (+3) and the Bank exchange (price rises with every purchase) — spend them on the SPHERE GRID: stats, passives, active skills AND every weapon's skill path (the paths CROSS at X nodes), GEMS for soul-gem sockets.
